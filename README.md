 👥 HR Attrition & Cost Analysis

A data analytics project analyzing employee attrition patterns and 
calculating the financial cost of employee turnover using IBM HR Dataset.

---

 🎯 Unique Angle — Cost of Attrition
Most HR reports show WHO is leaving.
This project shows HOW MUCH it's costing the company.

> Every employee who leaves costs 50% of their annual salary 
> in recruitment, training & lost productivity.

---

 📁 Dataset
- Source: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset (Kaggle)
- Records: 1,470 employees
- Columns used: Age, Department, Attrition, MonthlyIncome, 
  OverTime, YearsAtCompany, JobRole

---

 🔍 Steps Performed
1. Loaded CSV data using Pandas
2. Checked missing values — dataset was clean (0 missing)
3. Calculated overall attrition rate (16.1%)
4. Analyzed attrition by Department, Age Group, Overtime, Salary
5. Calculated Cost of Attrition using industry standard (50% rule)
6. Created 4 visualizations using Matplotlib
7. Imported data into MySQL database (1,470 rows)
8. Wrote 5 SQL queries for deeper analysis
9. Built dashboard website using HTML & CSS (Sidebar layout)

---

 📈 Key Findings
- Sales department has highest attrition — 20.6%
- Employees working overtime are 3x more likely to leave
- Employees who left earned $2,046 less/month than those who stayed
- Age 18-25 group has highest attrition — 34.7%
- Company lost $57.3M due to attrition

---

 💡 Business Recommendations
1. Reduce overtime in Sales — could save $3.2M in attrition costs
2. Salary review for junior roles — 15% hike can reduce turnover
3. Young employee retention program — mentorship for 18-25 age group
